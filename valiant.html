VCII and VALIANT

Partners in AI at Vanderbilt.

Both centers advance AI. VALIANT drives translational AI across every field at Vanderbilt. VCII focuses on one: intelligent imaging through AI at Vanderbilt Health.

VALIANT brings

A Discovery Vanderbilt center for translational AI

Breadth
Translational AI across medicine, materials, humanities, and education.
Community
12 labs and 100+ AI Fellows and Scholars across schools.
Methods and reach
New AI methods, plus regional and national partnerships.

VCII brings

A Vanderbilt Health center within VUIIS

Imaging depth
AI for how scans are acquired, read, and used, across 12+ modalities at VUIIS.
Medical RSI
Every patient outcome improves the models, protocols, and next scan.
Clinical workflow
Closes the loop of imaging, AI, and healthcare at Vanderbilt Health.

How we work together

Methods to clinic
AI methods from both centers reach Vanderbilt Health imaging data.
Clinic to questions
Clinical results raise the next questions for both centers.
Questions to methods
The answers sharpen the next method.
Shared leadership
VALIANT Director Bennett A. Landman sits on the VCII Advisory Board.
Open collaboration
Joint proposals and multi-center imaging data with partner institutes.
