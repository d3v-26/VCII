Meet our people.

The founding directors and the advisory board guiding VCII's launch.

Founding director

Ruogu Fang, Ph.D.

Dr. Fang develops medical AI for brain health and neuroimaging, with work spanning earlier diagnosis, personalized outcomes, and brain-inspired intelligence.

Founding Director, VCII · Flowers Family Dean's Faculty Fellow in Engineering, Biomedical Engineering. Joins Vanderbilt from the University of Florida, where she directed the SMILE Lab and served as Associate Director of the Intelligent Critical Care Center.

Official portrait via Vanderbilt School of Engineering.

Founding director

John Gore, Ph.D.

Dr. Gore directs the Vanderbilt University Institute of Imaging Science, with four decades of work spanning the physics of MRI signal formation and its biomedical applications.

Director, Vanderbilt University Institute of Imaging Science · Hertha Ramsey Cress Chair in Medicine · University Distinguished Professor, Radiology and Radiological Sciences, Biomedical Engineering, and Physics and Astronomy. Member, National Academy of Engineering.
