Meet our people.

The founding directors and the advisory board guiding VCII's launch. Together, the founding directors have led more than $140M in career awards and grants.

Founding directors

John C. Gore, PhD

Founding Director, VCII

MRI physics · functional MRI · white-matter signals

Dr. Gore directs the Vanderbilt University Institute of Imaging Science. His work spans the physics of MRI signal formation, functional MRI, including signals in white matter, and their biomedical applications.

Appointments

  • Director, Vanderbilt University Institute of Imaging Science
  • Hertha Ramsey Cress Chair in Medicine
  • Professor of Radiology & Radiological Sciences
  • Vice Chair of Research

Honors

  • Member, National Academy of Engineering
  • Fellow, National Academy of Inventors
  • ISMRM Gold Medal
EmailOfficial profilePublications

Ruogu Fang, PhD

Founding Director, VCII

AI for brain health · foundation and agentic models · digital twins

Dr. Fang develops AI for brain health, brain-inspired AI, and foundation and agentic models for medical imaging, with a top-1% ranking in image analysis (ScholarGPS).

Appointments

  • Professor of Biomedical Engineering
  • Flowers Family Dean's Faculty Fellow
  • Professor (affiliated) of Electrical & Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Radiology, Biomedical Informatics

Honors

  • National Academy of Medicine Emerging Leader in Health and Medicine Scholar
  • NAI Senior Member
  • MICCAI 2027 Challenge Chair
  • Associate Editor, Medical Image Analysis
